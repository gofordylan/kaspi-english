# Kaspi.kz app: Russian → English cheat sheet
*Compiled 17 Oct 2026 (Asia/Almaty). The app's interface is only in Kazakh (KK) and Russian (RU). Keep the app in **Russian**: Apple's Translate app and Live Text don't support Kazakh, and Google's tools handle Russian well.*

**How to read the status column**
- ✅ **Verified**: the exact label appears in an official Kaspi source (Kaspi Guide at guide.kaspi.kz, the kaspi.kz website, or the App Store / Google Play listing).
- 🌐 **Site label**: the exact wording appears on the kaspi.kz website's description of the app feature. The app very likely uses the same words, but I couldn't check the app screen itself.
- ❓ **Unverified**: a common word you'll probably see, but I couldn't confirm it as an exact on-screen label.

## Safety reminders
- Install Kaspi only from the App Store, Google Play or AppGallery, and check that the developer is Kaspi. Never install a "Kaspi.apk", a "modded/English Kaspi", or an "update" sent by link. These are malware, and Kaspi blocks apps installed from unknown sources anyway.
- Remote-access apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, HopToDesk), root or jailbreak will cause Kaspi to refuse to run.
- Kaspi staff never ask for your SMS code. Official codes come only by SMS from "kaspi.kz". Fraud hotline: 116.

*Note on Kasper: news reports and the CEO say it understands Kazakh, Russian, English and Turkish, but the Kaspi Guide FAQ (q19254) still says Kazakh and Russian only. Try English. If it doesn't respond, write in Russian.*
